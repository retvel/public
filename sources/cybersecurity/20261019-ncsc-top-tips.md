## Our top tips are:

### [Protect your email by using a strong and separate password](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/use-a-strong-and-separate-password-for-email)

Cyber criminals can use your email to access many of your personal accounts, leaving you vulnerable to identity theft.

### [Install the latest software and app updates](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/install-the-latest-software-and-app-updates)

Software and app updates contain vital security updates to help protect your devices from cyber criminals.

### [Turn on 2-step verification (2SV)](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/activate-2-step-verification-on-your-email)

2-step verification is recommended to help protect your online accounts.

### [Password managers: how they help you secure passwords](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/password-managers)

Using a password manager can help you create and remember passwords.

### [Backing up your data](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/always-back-up-your-most-important-data)

Safeguard your most important data, such as your photos and key documents, by backing them up to an external hard drive or a cloud-based storage system.

### [Three random words](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/three-random-words)

Use three random words to create a password that's difficult to crack.
