# CySA+

CompTIA CySA+ validates your ability to detect, analyze, and respond to cybersecurity threats in today’s security environments. For the most up-to-date skills and content, choose CySA+ V4 (New Version). CySA+ V3 (Retiring Version) remains available, with English learning products retiring November 22, 2026, the English exam retiring December 22, 2026, and translations retiring March 23, 2027.
