## Install the latest software and app updates

<img src="/sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_2xs/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=WKUnWde2" class="w-full h-full object-cover" loading="eager" srcset="/sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_2xs/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=WKUnWde2 200w, /sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_xs/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=Vc8L0hZC 400w, /sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_sm/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=qMmOlWHW 800w, /sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_md/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=65fMd0Lj 1000w, /sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_lg/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=fJz3Q_S3 1500w, /sites/default/files/styles/uncropped_huge/public/images/Updates.jpg?itok=ZTj9q52w 2000w" sizes="auto" width="200" height="133" alt="Computer screen with the option of updating the software or turning on automatic updates" />

Image credit: iStock.com/LPETTET

Applying security updates promptly will help protect your devices and accounts from cyber criminals.

You should apply updates to your apps and your device's software as soon as they are available. Updates include protection from viruses and other kinds of malware, and will often include improvements and new features.

If you receive a prompt to update your device (or apps), don’t ignore it. Applying these updates is one of the most important (and quickest) things you can do to keep yourself safe online.

You should also turn on 'automatic updates' in your device's settings, if available. This will mean you do not have to remember to apply updates.

### Note that:

- updating your device may take some time and requires a reliable internet connection, so it’s best to do it at home where you can access your wi-fi (and keep your device plugged in)
- [older devices](/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/install-the-latest-software-and-app-updates#section_3) will eventually stop receiving updates updates

## When your device no longer receives updates

Manufacturers eventually stop providing updates for older devices. If you continue to use a device that’s no longer supported:

- ### it won't receive updates that contain new features and performance improvements

- ### it won't receive the security updates from the manufacturer (and without these your phone is less secure)

  

  

You don't need to buy the latest (or most expensive) model to stay safe, but if possible, **avoid** buying and using phones that are no longer supported. You can check online to find if a specific model still receives updates from the manufacturer. Here are the details for iPhone, Chrome, and Pixel/Nexus devices.

- [Supported iPhone models](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/iphe3fa5df43/ios)
- [Chrome OS (e.g. Chromebooks)](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/6220366?hl=en)
- [Pixel devices](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/4457705#when_updates)

If you have another type Android device (such as those manufactured by Samsung or Huawei), you'll have to[ check with the manufacturer.](https://support.google.com/android/answer/3094742)
